# Gestural interface

The user requested a classical white and black interface with concise copy,
simple cards, modest type, and small fade or slide transitions. No pricing,
glow, orbs, gradients, decorative clocks, section numbers, or oversized headings.

The app is a task-focused practice workspace. Use white backgrounds, charcoal
text, grey secondary text and thin grey borders. System sans-serif typography,
14–15px controls/body copy, 25–32px page headings. Use weight and spacing for
hierarchy. Primary actions are black buttons; secondary actions are outlined.
Keep focus visible and honour reduced-motion preferences.

Navigation has Gestural on the left, Home / Albums / Scripts in the centre,
and Log in or Account on the right. Keep these destinations stable on mobile.
Home gives direct access to practice and writing. Albums contains creation,
recordings, and progress reviews. Scripts owns generation and saved scripts.
Recording has its own page, with album and script context carried in the URL.
Account contains identity and logout. Old URLs redirect to their replacements.

Desktop pages use a 1040px content area with 32px gutters. Mobile has 20px
gutters and stacked work areas. Albums use plain bordered cards. Script lines
and delivery cues use aligned columns. Prefer full pages over detail modals.
Use a single short page entrance; no looping decorative animation.

Loading, empty, error, disabled, and pending states are explicit. Camera access
starts on user action. Preserve recording shutdown, ownership checks, saved
reviews, script generation, and album/session management.

Signed-out Home showcases the core workflow. Albums and Scripts show clearly
labelled, selectable illustrative examples with account creation and login links.
Public previews never fetch private workspace data; recording and real detail
pages still require authentication.
